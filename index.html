Research

PhD scholar, University of Mumbai. Project guide: Dr. Sajeev Chacko.

Computational study of the reactivity of molecular oxygen and carbon monoxide with nanoparticles

Catalysts decide how fast and how cleanly a reaction runs. I model Mn–Ni bimetallic nanoparticles on the computer and study how O₂ and CO molecules adsorb on them, to understand which surface arrangements bind these gases most strongly.

The work involves building atomic structures, running simulations of atoms and molecules, and interpreting the results. I presented the project during the final year of my MSc, and I attended a one-week workshop on DFT and Molecular Dynamics in December 2022.
